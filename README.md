# 🏢 LOGS

LOGS is an internal iOS application designed to support the **Engineering Division of Ciputra World Surabaya** by streamlining asset and inventory management.

The app centralizes asset tracking, visual monitoring, and maintenance history into a single, easy-to-use system, helping engineers manage facilities more efficiently and accurately.

---

## 📋 Overview

LOGS helps the engineering team keep track of physical assets across the building by providing:
- A structured inventory system
- A visual map showing asset locations and statuses
- Digital maintenance records that replace manual maintenance cards

---

## 🔧 Key Features

- 📦 **Inventory Management**  
  Track assets, equipment, and facilities in a centralized system.

- 🗺️ **Visual Asset Map**  
  View assets on a visual map with real-time status indicators to quickly identify issues or maintenance needs.

- 📝 **Maintenance History Logs**  
  Input and store maintenance history directly in the app, creating a digital maintenance card for each asset.

- ⚙️ **Status Tracking**  
  Monitor asset conditions such as active, under maintenance, or requiring attention.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

LOGS was built to improve operational efficiency by reducing manual record-keeping, improving visibility across assets, and ensuring maintenance data is easily accessible to the engineering team.

---

## 🧑‍💻 Tech Stack

- Platform: iOS
- Language: Swift
- Frameworks: SwiftUI
- Target Users: Engineering Division – Ciputra World Surabaya
